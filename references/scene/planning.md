# Scene planning

Choose one mode per image brief: `真实使用场景`, `产品肖像`, or `细节证据`.

Use a real scene only when placement, interaction, fit, workflow, storage behavior, lifestyle, or ownership context explains the selling point. Define a named context, placement/use relationship, spatial anchors, scale references, supporting elements, coordination logic, and fact boundary.

Use product portrait for whole-product form, proportion, color, or overall design. Use detail evidence for material, finish, construction, mechanism, interface, texture, ingredient, or workmanship. Do not force every screen into a room.

Reject generic stages described as homes, products floating in undefined rooms, decorative props unrelated to use, conflicting materials, unsupported functions, and scenes that weaken the intended proof.
