# S4 scene-fit audit

For a real usage scene, PASS requires the declared context, placement/use relationship, anchors, scale cues, supporting elements, and coordination logic to be visibly credible. Reject a product that appears pasted into an unrelated set.

For product portrait or detail evidence, verify that the result honestly remains that mode and does not claim documentary use. Reject product distortion, floating, implausible support, heavy occlusion, background competition, or dramatic stage lighting that contradicts the declared mode.
