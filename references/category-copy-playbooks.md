# Category copy playbooks

Read only the section matching the current product category. Product evidence and applicable regulations always override this reference.

## Furniture and home

Lead with the product's role in a room, followed by verified material, structure, dimensions, storage, or daily-use detail. A good result is a concrete selection or use outcome: whether it fits a location, how items are organized, or how an evidenced finish and form set the intended visual tone. Never infer load-bearing, formaldehyde, safety, durability, or ergonomic performance without proof.

## Beauty

Lead with use moment and texture, then verified ingredient, formula, certification, or routine evidence. Describe use experience only within supported cosmetic wording; do not imply medical treatment or unproven efficacy.

## 3C

Lead with the task or setting, then verified function, specification, compatibility, or interface. Make compatibility and performance conditional where evidence is limited; never invent battery, speed, protection, or ecosystem claims.

## Food

Lead with taste, eating occasion, ingredient, or process evidence. Use only verified ingredients, origin, processing, storage, and nutrition language; avoid medical, therapeutic, or absolute health claims.

## Maternal and health-related products

Use the most conservative factual wording. Lead with verified specification, applicable age or use condition, and required compliance information. Do not write safety, developmental, medical, prevention, or treatment claims unless the exact evidence and legal basis permit them.
