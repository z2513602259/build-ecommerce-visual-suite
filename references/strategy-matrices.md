# Platform, task, and category strategy baselines

These matrices are starting points, not facts. Apply current product evidence and explicit user requirements first, then use live research to calibrate them.

## Platform baseline

| Platform | Typical decision behavior | Visual and copy baseline |
|---|---|---|
| JD | Parameter comparison, trust, delivery, specification clarity | Higher information density, structured proof, clear parameters, restrained claims |
| Tmall / Taobao | Brand impression plus conversion sequence | Strong first-screen hook, progressive benefits, scene and proof balance, promotional information only in post-layout |
| Xiaohongshu | Discovery, taste, lived experience, shareability | Editorial or lifestyle feeling, lower hard-sell pressure, authentic scenarios, concise benefit language |
| Douyin / Kuaishou | Fast attention and immediate comprehension | Strong visual hook, single-focus frames, short copy units, motion-friendly composition |
| Amazon / Shopify | Fast scanning, comparison, specification and trust | Clean product visibility, modular benefits, clear compatibility/specification, restrained lifestyle support |

Record research-supported deviations rather than forcing the baseline.

## Task profiles

- `DETAIL_PAGE`: progressive multi-screen conversion narrative with complete copy and independent image tasks.
- `MAIN_IMAGE`: product-first, fast recognition, platform-safe composition, one dominant purchase reason per image.
- `PROMO_POSTER`: campaign hierarchy and atmosphere; price, promotion, and CTA remain post-layout data.

Read `task-profiles.md` for counts, ratios, copy units, and asset behavior.

## Category baseline

| Category | High-value evidence | Main compliance risk |
|---|---|---|
| Furniture / home / textile | Material, structure, craft, dimensions, storage, touch, scene fit | Converting visible form into safety, durability, environmental, or ergonomic promises |
| Beauty / fragrance | Ingredients, texture, packaging, use ritual, certified test evidence | Medical, guaranteed efficacy, absolute safety, or unsupported ingredient performance |
| Food / beverage | Ingredients, origin, process, flavor, serving scene, packaging | Disease claims, exaggerated nutrition, unsupported origin or quality grading |
| Digital / appliances / 3C | Verified specifications, compatibility, interface, function, certified performance | Invented benchmarks, battery life, compatibility, waterproofing, or certifications |
| Maternal / child / safety | Materials, construction, age/size applicability, verified standards | Absolute safety, zero-risk, medical, developmental, or unsupported certification claims |
| Apparel / accessories | Fabric, cut, construction, detail, sizing, styling scenarios | Unsupported functional fabric, body-shaping, origin, or durability claims |
| Health-related care | Ingredients, usage, packaging, lawful approved wording | Treatment, prevention, guaranteed effect, drug-equivalent, or diagnostic claims |

When a category has regulatory sensitivity, prefer exact source language and flag any claim needing legal review.

## Precedence and conflict record

When live evidence conflicts with a baseline, retain the evidence, record `matrix_deviation`, and state why. Never overwrite an explicit user requirement or verified product fact with a matrix recommendation.

In S3A, options must differ in at least three substantive dimensions: conversion emphasis, worldview, palette, light, layout, scene-mode distribution, target context, or proof priority. A label rewrite or background-color change alone is not a distinct option.
