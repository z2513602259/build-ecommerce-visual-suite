# Consumer copy quality

Use this reference in S2 and S3A. It applies to final consumer-facing copy only; evidence locations and audit notes stay internal.

## Conversion standard

Every approved point and every copy unit must answer a real consumer decision question. Build copy from a verified fact, a concrete result in the buyer's use or selection context, and a clear reason to continue considering the product. A phrase such as `更清楚` or `更有质感` is not a benefit by itself: it needs a specific object, context, or result.

Use the replacement test: if a line could be pasted onto any comparable product without changing meaning, rewrite it with the current product's verified material, structure, dimension, function, or visible detail. Do not turn a fact into an unverified performance promise.

## S2 candidate decision map

Each candidate additionally contains:

- `消费者决策问题`: the question the point helps answer.
- `利益类型`: a category-appropriate value such as visual definition, fit, convenience, quality judgment, or risk removal.
- `具体消费者结果`: a restrained, concrete result of the verified fact.
- `表达边界`: claims and inferences that must not be made without stronger evidence.

These fields are approved together with the point and must be retained into S3.

## S3A narrative and quality record

Each option contains `购买叙事` with `核心购买主张`, `首屏承诺`, `主要购买顾虑`, `决策递进`, and `文案气质`. The sequence should move from desire or positioning, to proof, fit, concern removal, and an action cue.

Each option also contains a final `文案质检` result. It records only conclusions and edits, never hidden reasoning. Its `单元检查` covers every screen or content unit and includes its consumer decision question, fact anchor, `产品专属性` (`HIGH` for final copy), clear purchase-reason status, generic-copy risks, duplicate risks, and correction record. Final S3A copy may proceed only when the status is `PASS` and unresolved generic or duplicate risks are empty.
