# S1 product identity lock

Build the lock only from current-product images. Record angle-specific references, visible feature IDs, immutable features, allowed and conditional changes, forbidden inference, and unresolved risks.

Use `READY` only when at least one primary view and all critical visible features are documented. Furniture checks include silhouette, proportions, panel or drawer count, gaps, handles, feet or base, edge profiles, material color, grain direction, finish, hardware, and markings. Adapt the checklist to other categories.

Exact grain, print, logo, label, interface, artwork, or serial markings require real-pixel compositing or a locked product region later.
