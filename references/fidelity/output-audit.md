# S4 product-fidelity audit

Check structure, proportions, part count, handles and hardware, edges and supports, material and color, texture direction, fixed markings, and reference-angle match.

Each applicable item is PASS with a concrete note. Use NOT_APPLICABLE only when the category or view genuinely lacks that feature. Any FAIL or PENDING rejects the generated asset.

Reject unsupported viewpoints, generated-image reference chains, omitted critical features, altered product geometry, and pixel-specific texture or markings recreated by reference generation.
