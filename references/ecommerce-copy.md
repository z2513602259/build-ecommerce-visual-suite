# Ecommerce conversion copy

Use this reference in S2 for `可转化利益` and in S3 for every consumer-visible copy field. The required mode is `电商转化型`: concise, concrete, scannable, evidence-bounded, and oriented toward purchase decisions.

## Core sequence

Build each unit in this order:

1. **Purchase conclusion**: what should the consumer understand immediately?
2. **Product fact or difference**: what concrete material, structure, number, function, fit, or design supports it?
3. **Consumer outcome**: what becomes clearer, easier, tidier, more suitable, more comfortable, or more confident?
4. **Decision cue**: what should the consumer check, compare, understand, or decide next?

Do not turn this sequence into a paragraph. Each field carries one distinct job.

## Field duties and density

| Field | Duty | Default density |
|---|---|---|
| `页面职责` | Internal planning label; never typeset as copy | No consumer-language requirement |
| `主标题` | Purchase conclusion or strongest reason to care | At most 10 visible characters |
| `副标题` | Supporting fact, difference, number, material, or benefit | At most 10 visible characters |
| `卖点短句` | Direct consumer outcome | 6–22 visible characters |
| `说明文案` | One verified fact plus one restrained consumer benefit | Normally 16–42 visible characters, one or two sentences |
| `CTA或承接语` | Decision cue or natural transition | At most 14 visible characters |
| `对应的真实产品依据` | Internal provenance only | Never typeset |

Record a justified override when legal accuracy, platform format, language, or category terminology requires another density. Do not use an override merely to preserve verbose prose.

## Preferred patterns

- Fact first: `五层抽屉独立分区，不同物品更好分类，日常取放更清楚。`
- Difference first: `无指接、无贴皮、无复合，用料构成更直接，购买判断更明白。`
- Fit first: `750×450×1100mm，下单前先核对摆放位置，减少尺寸不合适。`
- Appearance benefit: `深暖木色搭配自然纹理，整体观感沉稳，日常更耐看。`

Use restrained outcomes. Do not invent capacity, durability, safety, comfort, environmental, health, or performance benefits.

## Reject these modes

### Brand essay or literary copy

Reject mood as the main selling point: `深木入境`, `木纹自有深度`, `边缘有光`, `诗意栖居`, `为空间写下叙事`.

### Curatorial or design-review copy

Reject commentary that sounds like a design article: `成为空间底色`, `形成视觉节奏`, `构成克制的观看层次`, `以体块进入空间`.

### Evidence-reporting copy

Reject narration of the production or audit process: `把真实信息一次看清`, `依次呈现已确认的产品事实`, `名称清楚`, `本页已整理`, `集中确认`, `原图可见`.

### Mechanical document navigation

Reject repetitive transitions such as `继续看材质与细节`, `逐项查看`, `回看产品信息`, `回到空间场景`, `放大边缘细节`, or `完成产品确认`.

Use decision-oriented alternatives such as `先看用料`, `再看怎么收`, `下单前量一量`, `尺寸合适再决定`, or another natural cue supported by the unit.

## A/B/C differentiation

All options remain `电商转化型`:

- A may emphasize direct facts and fast comprehension.
- B may emphasize quality evidence and purchase confidence.
- C may emphasize usage outcomes and fit.

Visual style, tone, and emphasis may differ. No option may become a brand essay, poetic campaign, curatorial statement, or design criticism.

## Final self-check

For every unit, verify:

- a consumer can understand the purchase point in one glance
- the title is a conclusion rather than atmosphere
- the subtitle adds a fact or benefit rather than extending a metaphor
- the selling-point line names a consumer outcome
- the explanation contains a concrete fact and a restrained benefit
- the CTA/transition helps a decision instead of navigating a document
- no evidence source, production step, or internal reasoning appears in visible copy
- titles, subtitles, selling-point lines, and explanations are not duplicated across screens or units in the same option
- A/B/C differ in conversion emphasis without changing approved product truth

## Product-specific conversion check

Before finalizing a line, apply the replacement test: if it can describe any comparable product without changing a word, add a current-product fact anchor and a specific selection or use result. Do not use a generic phrase as the entire benefit, including `更清楚`, `更明白`, `更直接`, `更易搭配`, `方便核对`, `更有质感`, `更加高级`, `观感更好`, or `层次更丰富`.

Titles must state a purchase conclusion, not a section label such as `材质`, `尺寸`, `细节`, `结构`, `参数`, or `卖点`. Keep the visible copy within its existing character limits. Record the internal consumer decision question and fact anchor in the S3A copy-quality result; never typeset those audit fields in the image.
